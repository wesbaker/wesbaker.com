A Discord bot for managing a book club rotation. Randomizes who picks the book each month, tracks book picks with Goodreads integration, and sends mid-month reminders.

<Figure src={pick} alt="/pick command">
  The `/pick` command lets the book chooser pick their book or the admin to set
  a book for someone else.
</Figure>

<Figure src={reminder} alt="Monthly reminder message">
  Every month a reminder is sent to the current chooser and next month's as
  well.
</Figure>

<Figure src={schedule} alt="/schedule command">
  The `/schedule` command shows the upcoming schedule along with anyone who's
  been pinned to a specific month.
</Figure>
